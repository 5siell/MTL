Australia

Australia is a unique template in that:

  • There are vital mega bonuses.
  • Some regions are significantly better than others.
  • Most bonuses border through single-border connections.

As a result of the latter (the prevalence of single-border connections), games tend to be decided on picks and you can utilize defensive kill-rates to absorb early pressure and converting into a win.
