Succession Wars

Succession Wars is a Local Deployment template with very low income and few armies. With so few armies on the board, being close to your income and using defensive kill rates decide a lot of games.

Settings

  • Local Deployment: income from a bonus can only be deployed inside that bonus.
  • Standard kill rates (60% offense, 70% defense).
  • 7 starting territories with 4 armies each.
  • Cyclic move order.
  • No base income. Income comes from bonuses and from the number of territories you control.
  • Extra income per territory count: +1 for every 5 territories (5 → +1, 10 → +2, 15 → +3, and so on).

Map Overview / Dynamics

The map has small clusters of income spread across the center. Because of Local Deployment, the armies a bonus produces stay inside it, so how close your income is to the fighting matters a lot. Armies are scarce and defending is cheaper than attacking, so holding chokepoints is a real option.

Picking

There are two main ideas when picking:

  1. Take the whole cluster and overrun the opponent with an early army advantage. The game is decided in the early game. Examples: game 1, game 2.
  2. Pick so you can keep expanding while holding chokepoints, and win the late game. In this game I just try to survive in the south and north while expanding, since my win condition is the late game.

Both are hard to pull off. My advice is to stick to the principles and pick useful territories.

Gameplay

Pay close attention to how many territories you control. Since the template gives extra income for every 5 territories, the thresholds are worth planning around. In this game I came back in the midgame by reaching 15 territories for +3 income, together with some defensive kill-rate tricks.
