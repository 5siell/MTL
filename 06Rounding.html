Rounding

This section is for me asking Optimum every other week whether sanction rounds up or down. I'll try to include every rounding interaction here for fast tracing. Forum for reference.

Sanctions

Depends how you see it:

  • The fractional penalty rounds DOWN.
  • Overall it rounds UP.
  • Turn 5. Rufus sanctions PS.

    One way to look at it:

    1. PS was at 10 income before sanctions.
    2. Sanctions hit 25% -> 2.5. PS' income rounded to 10 - 2.5 = 7.5.
    3. 7.5 becomes 8. Sanctions round UP.

    Another way to look at it:

    1. PS was at 10 income before sanctions.
    2. Sanctions hit 25% -> 2.5. The fractional penalty of 2.5 rounds to 2. It rounds DOWN.
    3. Final income is 10 - 2 = 8.

In the Forum it says it rounds down; to me it always rounded up. You can find tables for sanctions on the Cards section.

Reinforcement Card

Rounds DOWN

  • Exception: Anything less than 1 army always rounds UP; you don't get an RF of +0.
  • RF Card rounds DOWN.
  • No example for it; in Tarabonia's Switch it's 40%, no 0.5.
  • 50% RF for 5 turns = 2.5 -> +2 RF card.

Emergency Blockade & Blockade

Rounds DOWN

  • Turn 6. 250% blockade at 3 armies. 3 -> 7.5. Rounds DOWN to 7. Same for Emergency Blockade!

Bomb

Rounds DOWN

  • Bombing 5 armies -> 5 × 50% = 2.5 -> Rounds to 2. Kills Extra.

Kill Rates

Rounds UP

  • 5 Defenders at 70% kill rate kill 3.5 -> 4 attackers.
  • 2 Attackers at 75% kill rate kill 1.5 -> 2 defenders.

Attack by Percentage

Rounds DOWN

  • 5 movable armies and 50% attack -> 5 × 50% = 2.5 -> Rounds down to 2.
